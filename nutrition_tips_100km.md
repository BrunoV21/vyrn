# Nutrition Tips — 100 km / 1000 m Elevation Gain

*Context: Coruche, ~26°C high, possible light rain. Duration estimate: 4–6 hours saddle time.*

---

## General Rule of Thumb

- **Carbs:** 60–90 g per hour after the first hour. Closer to 60–70 g/hr if you're not gut-trained; push toward 80–90 g/hr if you are.
- **Fluids:** 500–750 ml per hour. More if it's hot or you're a heavy sweater.
- **Sodium:** 500–1000 mg per hour (roughly 1 large pinch of salt per bottle, or use electrolyte tabs).
- **Don't wait until you're hungry or thirsty** — by then it's too late.

---

## Pre-Ride (2–3 hours before)

- Meal rich in **complex carbs**: oatmeal with banana & honey, or toast with jam, or rice/pasta.
- ~1.5 g carbs per kg body weight.
- **Hydrate** well — aim for 500 ml of water + electrolytes in the 2 hours before.
- Small coffee if you usually do (caffeine helps perceived effort).

---

## During the Ride — Hourly Schedule

| Hour | Carbs (g) | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0–1  | 30–40    | Sip electrolyte drink, eat a banana or energy bar early |
| 1–2  | 60–70    | Gel + energy bar, or 2 bananas + sports drink |
| 2–3  | 70–80    | This is the elevation section — gels are easiest to digest on climbs |
| 3–4  | 70–80    | Rice cake / sandwich + sports drink |
| 4–5  | 60–70    | Coke + bar, or gel + banana |
| 5–6  | 50–60    | Whatever you can stomach — gels, chews, Coke |

### Practical Carry List (for ~5 hrs)

- 3–4 energy gels
- 2–3 bars (or rice cakes)
- 2 bananas
- 1 small bag of gummy bears / jelly beans (quick sugar for the last hour)
- 2 bottles (one water, one electrolyte/carb mix)
- Optional: a small flask of flat Coke — great late-ride energy + caffeine

---

## Key Points for 1000 m Elevation

- The climbing sections will spike your energy expenditure. **Eat before the climbs**, not during the steepest parts (hard to digest under high intensity).
- On long descents your effort drops — good time to eat solid food while your stomach has blood flow.
- If you bonk (sudden energy crash, heavy legs, brain fog): **stop, eat a gel + drink, wait 10–15 min**. Don't push through.

---

## Hydration in ~26°C + Possible Rain

- Even with clouds and light rain, you'll still sweat. Rain can trick you into under-drinking.
- Alternate: one bottle plain water, one bottle with **electrolyte + carb** mix (e.g., 60–80 g carbs per 750 ml).
- If urine is dark yellow mid-ride, you're behind — drink more.

---

## Post-Ride Recovery (within 30–60 min)

- **Carbs + protein** in a ~3:1 or 4:1 ratio.
- Examples: chocolate milk (excellent), recovery shake, rice + chicken, or a sandwich.
- Rehydrate: 500 ml per 0.5 kg of body weight lost (weigh yourself if possible).
- Add some **salt** to your recovery meal.

---

## Quick Cheat Sheet

1. Eat **60��90 g carbs/hr** — start at minute 60, not when you feel empty.
2. Drink **500–750 ml/hr** — one bottle per hour minimum.
3. **Electrolytes** matter as much as water.
4. **Eat before climbs**, sip on descents.
5. If in doubt, **eat more** — running out of fuel ruins a good ride.
6. Practice your nutrition on training rides, don't try new foods on the big day.

---

*Ride smart, eat often, stay hydrated. Bom passeio!*
